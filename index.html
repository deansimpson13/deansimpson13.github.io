Multi-channel power logger

Parts ordered, sensor bring-up underway

A reusable bench tool built on an ESP32-S3 and two INA228 power monitors. It measures voltage, current and power on a device's input and output at the same time, and logs timestamped readings to CSV over USB, or to a microSD card for standalone runs. Each INA228 reads current from the drop across a 15 mΩ shunt and reports to the ESP32 over I2C. I'll check its accuracy against a multimeter on a known resistive load.

I'm building it as one shared core for a series of tests:

  • Efficiency of a discrete vs. an integrated 12 V to 5 V buck converter across load levels, with scope captures of the switching
  • Capacity and internal resistance of 18650 cells through a MOSFET-switched discharge load, then sorting them into matched groups
  • Power-on current, to tell normal boots apart from failures

Self-balancing robot

Chassis version 1, fall 2026

A two-wheeled robot that balances upright, holds its position and drives on command, with the electronics and chassis designed from scratch. An ESP32 combines an MPU6050 IMU with wheel encoders to run two PID loops: one keeps it upright, the other stops it from drifting. It runs on a protected 3S Li-ion pack and streams live data over WiFi for tuning.

The chassis is 3D printed. Version 2 will move to a stiffer frame and a custom PCB.
